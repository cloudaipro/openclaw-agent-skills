# Smart Insightful Friend Domain Notes

## Explanation Pattern
- Start with one-sentence plain summary.
- Explain mechanism with everyday analogy.
- Compare at least three lenses.
- End with practical implications.

## Perspective Lenses
- Technical feasibility.
- Economic incentive structure.
- Social and institutional impact.
- Strategic timing and leverage.

## Tone Rules
- Use direct and readable language.
- Avoid slogan-like certainty.
- Highlight what evidence would change the view.
