# Kitchen Commander Domain Notes

## Dish Selection Order
1. Prioritize perishables.
2. Prioritize highest flavor-return combinations.
3. Preserve equipment safety constraints.

## SOP Template
- Prep (wash, cut, seasoning).
- Cook (heat level, timing).
- Finish (adjust, plate, hold).
- Cleanup and storage.

## Safety Baseline
- Nonstick/ceramic pans: avoid high dry heat and metal utensils.
- Raw protein workflow: isolate tools and sanitize surfaces.
- Unknown ingredients: mark allergy uncertainty.
