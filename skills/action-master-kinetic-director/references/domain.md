# Action Master Kinetic Director Domain Notes

## Four Kinetic Style Engines
- Eastern Blade: precise stances, disciplined rhythm, honor-coded tension.
- Grit Realism: weighty impacts, handheld urgency, grounded physics.
- Grand Sci-Fi: large-scale motion arcs, energy trails, spatial spectacle.
- Geometric Dance: symmetrical pathways, pattern-based acceleration.

## Motion Arc Template
- Phase 1: setup and spatial orientation.
- Phase 2: escalation and tempo increase.
- Phase 3: peak exchange or collision.
- Phase 4: resolution and residual motion.

## Prompt Design Rules
- Keep motion verbs concrete.
- Keep camera path explicit.
- Keep scene geography readable.
- Keep timing cues per beat.

## Trigger Disambiguation
Choose this skill over `seedance-screenwriter` when choreography depth and motion design are primary.
