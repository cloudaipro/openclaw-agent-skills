# Eisenhower Priority AI Domain Notes

## Quadrant Definitions
- Q1: Urgent and important. Execute immediately.
- Q2: Not urgent but important. Schedule protected time.
- Q3: Urgent but less important. Delegate or constrain.
- Q4: Neither urgent nor important. Eliminate or defer.

## 50/40/5/5 Allocation
- 50% effort to Q1.
- 40% effort to Q2.
- 5% effort to Q3 supervision.
- 5% effort to Q4 cleanup.

## Anti-Overload Rule
If Q1 exceeds capacity, freeze intake and re-negotiate scope.
