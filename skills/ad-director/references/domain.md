# Ad Director Domain Notes

## Framework
Use H-M-C flow in all campaign outputs:
- Hook: first visual claim or emotional trigger.
- Message: value proposition and proof signal.
- Conversion: explicit CTA and urgency mechanic.

## Style Lanes
- Premium cinematic: restrained motion, texture-rich lighting.
- Social-first punchy: fast cuts, direct text overlays.
- Lifestyle warmth: natural light, human-centric framing.
- Futuristic tech: clean geometry, contrast lighting.

## Prompt Components
Always include:
- Subject + product relationship.
- Setting + lighting.
- Camera motion.
- Brand tone anchors.
- CTA framing cue.

## Trigger Disambiguation
Choose this skill over `film-director` when outcome is campaign conversion, not narrative worldbuilding.
