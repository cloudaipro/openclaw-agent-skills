# Course Producer Domain Notes

## Learning Product Ladder
- Free value: lead magnet or mini lesson.
- Entry offer: low-friction starter program.
- Core offer: full curriculum transformation.
- Advanced offer: cohort, mentorship, or consulting.

## Curriculum Design
- Each module has one outcome, one artifact, one assessment.
- Sequence from foundation to application.
- Include feedback loops at key milestones.

## Funnel Metrics
Track:
- Lead to intro conversion.
- Intro to core conversion.
- Completion and testimonial rates.
