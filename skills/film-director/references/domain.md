# Film Director Domain Notes

## Narrative Structure
Preferred sequence:
1. Premise and controlling idea.
2. Character objective and opposition.
3. Act progression and turn points.
4. Scene objectives and transitions.

## Cinematic Grammar
- Use shot intention labels: reveal, isolate, escalate, resolve.
- Keep lens and camera movement motivated by story beat.
- Track color and light as emotional metadata.

## Scene Card Template
- Scene purpose.
- Conflict shift.
- Camera strategy.
- Lighting and color cue.
- Exit beat into next scene.

## Trigger Disambiguation
Choose this skill over `ad-director` when the user asks for narrative treatment rather than campaign conversion assets.
